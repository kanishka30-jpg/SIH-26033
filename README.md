## Problem Title

SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creator's Organization

Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme

Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Proposed Solution

We propose an AI-powered platform called **AgriPrice Connect** that helps farmers find better selling opportunities by providing market price comparisons and connecting them directly with buyers.

The platform collects available crop price information from different markets and displays it to farmers. It helps farmers compare prices, identify markets offering better rates, and find nearby retailers, wholesalers, restaurants, and bulk buyers.

Using Artificial Intelligence, the platform analyses past prices, seasonal trends, and available market data to estimate possible price changes. Farmers can use these estimates to make informed selling decisions.

The platform also allows buyers to place bulk orders directly with farmers and helps coordinate transportation.

The main innovation is combining price comparison, AI-based price forecasting, and direct buyer connections in one platform. This can reduce farmers' dependence on intermediaries, improve their bargaining power, and help consumers access agricultural products at competitive prices.

## Technical Approach

The platform can be developed using the following technologies:

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- **Backend:** Python with Django.
- **Database:** MySQL or SQLite.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Python and Scikit-learn for analysing historical prices and estimating price trends.
- **Market Data:** Available agricultural market-price data from reliable sources.
- **Maps:** Mapping APIs to identify nearby buyers and assist with transportation planning.

**Implementation Process:**

1. Farmers register and enter their crop details and available quantities.
2. The platform displays available market prices for comparison.
3. AI analyses historical data to estimate price trends.
4. Farmers identify suitable markets and potential buyers.
5. Buyers place orders directly through the platform.
6. Transportation is coordinated to deliver the products.

**Flowchart:**

Farmer Registration → Crop Details → Market Price Comparison → AI Price Forecast → Buyer Selection → Order Placement → Delivery

## Feasibility and Viability

The platform is feasible using existing web development tools and machine learning techniques.

A basic prototype can demonstrate farmer registration, crop listings, market price comparison, price-trend predictions using sample data, and buyer connections.

**Potential Challenges:**
- Incomplete or outdated market-price data.
- Unpredictable changes in crop prices.
- Limited digital knowledge among farmers.
- Transportation costs and delivery difficulties.

**Solutions:**
- Use reliable, regularly updated market data wherever available.
- Present AI predictions as estimates rather than guaranteed prices.
- Provide a simple interface with regional-language support.
- Connect farmers with nearby buyers to reduce transportation expenses.

The platform can generate revenue through small transaction fees or optional services for bulk buyers.

## Impact and Benefits

- Helps farmers compare market prices before selling.
- Improves farmers' bargaining power and selling decisions.
- Reduces dependence on unnecessary intermediaries.
- Connects farmers directly with retailers and bulk buyers.
- Helps consumers access products at competitive prices.
- Encourages transparent pricing and more efficient agricultural trade.

Overall, AgriPrice Connect aims to make agricultural trade more transparent, improve farmers' earnings, and reduce unnecessary costs throughout the supply chain.
